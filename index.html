Nek'zali

Quick info:single target + add cleave, 2 phases + intermission, Bloodlust on pull or in Phase 2

The whole fight:

  • Soulcoil Rite is big raid-wide damage whenever the boss cast Soulcoil Ignite or if an add walks into the Soulwell
    • on Heroic, Soulcoil Rite increases the damage of future Soulcoil Rite's by 15%

Phase 1:

  • Restless Amani: a bunch of adds spawn that need to be grip, stunned, and killed before they get to the Soulwell
  • Essence Rend: players with an arrow above their head, run to the wall, then get dispelled to drop a pool
  • tanks & healers, run away from the boss whenever she cast Possession Barrage to reduce the damage to the raid.
    • on Heroic, tanks gain stacks of Hollowed and should taunt swap whenever the debuff falls off.
  • tanks, melee's from the boss cause stacks of Hollowed, reducing the healing you receive by 5% per stack.

Intermission:

  • kill both Echose of Jawae one at a time
  • Hungering Pyre: half the raid soaks the big red group soak and half stays out. Players who stay out get little red fire circles
    • on Heroic, put the fire circles on dead Amani corpses to burn them and stop them from resurrecting
  • healers, watch players with the Residual Toll debuff

Phase 2:

  • Phase 1 but now you get more Soulcoil Rites from the boss casting Invoke and the pools on the ground now move around randomly.
  • kill the boss before she gets to full energy and wipes the raid.

Entombed Sentinels

Quick info:1 phase, mostly single target, Bloodlust on pull

Before the fight:

  • split the raid evenly between the two bosses and keep them 40+ yards apart

Green boss:

  • Venom Coagulation: swap to the slime add and kill it before another one spawns
  • Toxic Droplets: run over the green droplets before they explode and hit the raid
  • tanks, Empowering Slam is a big tank hit + physical damage increase
  • on Heroic, droplets and Empowering Slam dodge the spikes that shoot back at the boss

Red boss:

  • Unstable Miasma: red side group soak
  • Blighted Blood: dispellable DoTs on several players
  • tanks, Bloodvenom Injection is a big hit + magic damage increase
  • on Heroic, anyone who soaked drops an expanding pool

Intermission:

  • the bosses come together, gain 99% damage reduction, and the lower-health boss heals
  • match players so the green orbs above your heads add up to exactly 4 (1 + 3) (2 + 2)
  • tanks swap bosses when the intermission ends and then it's back to the main phase.
[3]

Vashnik

Quick info:1 phase, multi-target, Bloodlust on pull

The whole fight:

  • position the boss between 2 fountains before he reaches full energy and cast Imbibe
  • Imbibe activates the 2 closest fountains, spawning their adds and giving players their unique debuffs
    • once the adds are dead, move to the next pair of fountains and repeat
      • the overlapping fountain gets stronger when it's activated twice in a row

Other mechanics:

  • tanks, swap after each Dripping Fangs
  • on Heroic, Malignant Catalyst is big raid-wide damage

Blood fountain:

  • single add that can't be CC'd and splits into smaller adds when it dies
  • players with a leech circle can't be healed until several other players stand inside their circle

Shadow fountain:

  • AoE down the little adds and dodge the swirlies they shoot when they die
  • targeted players get a healing absorb and repeatedly spawn swirlies at their feet

Fire fountain:

  • the 2 adds pulse raid-wide damage while they're alive and explode when they die
    • on Heroic, each add that dies also puts a stacking DoT on the raid
  • players with a big explosion circle, run away from the raid to reduce the damage
[4]

Lost Explorers

Quick info:1 phase, mostly 2 target cleave, Bloodlust on pull

The whole fight:

  • open Gebbo's boxes until you find the fish and use it before Mor'zahi reaches full energy
  • recommended fish order: Nama, Iku, Gebbo
  • on Heroic, keep Nama with either Iku or Gebbo, but never both or all 3 gain 99% damage reduction

Nama:

  • melee bait and dodge Shell Spin
  • Mighty Thud: split into 3 group soaks, then move out of the patches

Iku:

  • interrupt Icebound Flames
  • Blink Nova target runs away to reduce the raid-wide damage
  • Frostfire Volley: pair Fire with Frost circles, drop beside each other, then run into the opposite patch

Gebbo:

  • Throw Junk: dodge boxes and spread out to open them
  • drop the bomb near the edge, then bounce over the blast wave with a mushroom

Tanks:

  • cross-taunt Nama and Iku around 7 Shredding Shards stacks
  • keep Nama beside whichever turtle has more health and pull the lower health one away
[5]

Sszorak

Quick info:1 phase, full single target, Bloodlust on pull or during Dig In

The whole fight:

  • place 3 world markers opposite the 3 tornado clusters
  • Venomous Surge: 2 players take the green arrows to the markers and drop the green blobs
  • players targeted with white wind circles should pair up and point your line toward each other to collide when launched
  • Dig In (intermission): outer tornados push the raid. Get pushed into a blob so its knockback sends everyone back onto the platform
    • the boss takes 30% increased damage during Dig In
  • Tank frontal combo: Ravage (white) is soaked by a tank, Mutilate (green) is group soaked
  • Tanks taunt swap if Corroding Venom stacks get too high

Heroic:

  • repeat Mutilates hit much harder (split the raid into 2 soak groups if needed)
  • Venomous Surge drops poison patches; place them near the edge and keep the middle clear
[6]

Twin Fangs

Quick info:1 phase, two-target cleave, Bloodlust on pull

The whole fight:

  • the fight revolves around stacks of the Eternal Venom debuff.
    • getting hit by any poison mechanics gives additional stacks
    • on Heroic, 10 stacks instantly kills you
  • run over the green orbs before they explode
  • when mini snake adds spawn point their targeted lines away and stop moving
  • red circles move to the edge and wait for them to expire
    • on Heroic, red circles leave behind pools when they expire
  • Ravenous Feast: get into the 3-hit group soak to remove Venom stacks
  • on Heroic, split the raid into 3 groups for Ravenous Feast, one group per hit
  • tanks, soak all 3 Stone Breaker hits and swap after each set

Intermission:

  • move toward Ithraz while dodging Vexhul's rotating poison beam
  • big wash your feet here
  • after the beam finishes, the bosses regroup and the main phase repeats
[7]

Coiled Altar

Quick info:p1 + p2 single target, Bloodlust intermission, P3 2-target cleave

Assignments:

  • place world markers at both ends of the platform for tank frontals
  • 2 to 3 mobile players to collect and stack green orbs
  • Heroic: split raid into 2 Guillotine soak groups
  • Heroic: assign 2 to 3 interrupts for Wail of Terror in p2

Phase 1: Zul'jan

  • collect green orbs near a marker + tanks use Sever to destroy a few at a time
  • leftover orbs explode when Zul'jan dies (use healer cd's)
  • Heroic: each destroyed orb adds a stacking raid DoT
  • tanks, swap after every Sever
  • Guillotine: group soak near the edge + run away from the huge explosion
  • Heroic: alternate soak groups
  • dispel Venomfang Poisons

Phase 2: Malacrass

  • Dreadmarch: break the MC shields before players walk off the edge
  • bring fixate ghosts to the marker + stare at them to stop them + tank frontal destroys them
  • tanks, swap after every Soul Sever and collect your Soul Fragments
  • run away with the big purple circles + collect your Soul Fragments after they explode (Gloombomb)
  • Eternal Nightfall: break shield + interrupt the cast
  • Heroic: interrupt and kill the Spiteful Soulcoiler add

Intermission: Bloodlust

  • Bloodlust + blast Zul'jan while he takes 100% increased damage
  • run into some green Soul Fragments before they reach and heal Zul'jan
  • each fragment you stop damages the raid + use raid CDs

Phase 3: Both bosses:

  • stack green orbs + Dreadmarch ghosts for the tank frontal
  • tanks, swap after every frontal + collect Soul Fragments
  • heal through Zul'jan's huge raid-wide healing absorbs (Defilement of the Coiled Altar)
  • keep both bosses close in HP and kill them together
  • Heroic: Guillotine soaks force the raid back and forth across the platform
